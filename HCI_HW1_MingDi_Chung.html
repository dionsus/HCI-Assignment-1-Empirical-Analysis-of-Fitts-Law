4. When ready, lift your thumb from the OK button and tap Number 5 as fast as possible.
5. Tapping other numbers counts as an Error; tapping empty remote areas counts as a Miss. You must successfully tap 5 to complete the trial.
6. There are 90 trials in total, with rest breaks in between.
